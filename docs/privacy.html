Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy covers the private home-automation app that Spridgehub uses to connect a home's own Home Assistant to Google Nest (thermostat, doorbell and camera). It is a personal app used only by its owner, for their own home. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, through Google's Smart Device Management API: the thermostat's temperatures and settings, and the doorbell and camera's events and live view.

How it is used

Only to control the owner's own heating and to tell them who is at their door. The data is processed on a computer in the owner's home.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account, under Security, then Third-party apps and services.
